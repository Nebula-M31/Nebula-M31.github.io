Euler’s Circuit Theorem

（Seven bridges of Königsberg）

Eulerian Circuit：a closed walk cover all arcs.

Euler's Circuit Theorem

An undirected connected graph has an Eulerian circuit ⟺\Longleftrightarrow⟺ the degree of every node is even.

proof.

  • necessity：边的全覆盖 →\rightarrow→ 点的全覆盖；每个点：有进必有出 ⇒\Rightarrow⇒ 度为偶数。
  • sufficiency：
    • Lemma：undirected connected graph，every node’s degree is even ⇒\Rightarrow⇒ it contains a closed walk ⇒\Rightarrow⇒ it contains a cycle.
    • Induction：
      • GGG：undirected connected graph，∀\forall∀ nodes even degree
      • base：GGG with 2 edges has an Eulerian circuit
      • hypothesis：GGG with fewer than mmm edges has an Eulerian circuit
      • induction step：GGG with mmm edges
        1. 用 Lemma 找出一条 cycle CCC；
        2. G−C=G′G - C = G'G−C=G′；
        3. 对 G′G'G′ 的每个 component 用 hypothesis，可以得到不同的 Eulerian circuit；
        4. 把这些 Eulerian circuits 链接到 CCC；
        5. 形成一条 closed walk 就得到一个 Eulerian circuit。
